About

A personal research tool that watches for statements from the US president referencing specific companies, sectors, or investing keywords — then tracks how the mentioned stock actually moves over the following four trading days.

What this is / isn't

What this is

A way to catch market-relevant statements faster than checking the news manually, with real before-and-after price data attached to every mention.

What this isn't

A trading signal, a prediction engine, or financial advice. It reports what already happened — it doesn't forecast what happens next.

Sources monitored
  • Truth Social His own posts, via an unofficial third-party RSS mirror. Direct evidence — but the mirror can go down or change format without notice.
  • White House Official whitehouse.gov press release feed. Direct evidence, reliable source.
  • News wires Coverage pulled via NewsAPI. Indirect — someone reporting on him, not him speaking directly. Weighted lower.
How it works
  1. Scans all three sources once a day for anything matching a defined watchlist of companies and keywords.
  2. Every match is tagged direct or indirect — that distinction matters more than the headline itself.
  3. New mentions get their current price logged via Yahoo Finance data.
  4. The stock is tracked for 4 trading days, recomputing % change on every run.
  5. Results are emailed as a daily digest and published to this site.
Read before trusting this
Reactive, not predictive. By the time a mention is caught here, it's already public — other market participants are seeing the same information at the same time. Keyword matching is simple substring search, not true language understanding, and will occasionally misfire on coincidental word matches. A confirmed multi-day trend has usually already been priced into the stock by the time it's "confirmed." Nothing on this site should be treated as investment advice.
