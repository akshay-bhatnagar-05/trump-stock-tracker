WIRE DESK

Tracks direct statements (Truth Social, White House) and indirect news coverage that name a stock on your watchlist, then follows the price for four trading days so you can judge the reaction yourself. About this project →

Direct — his own channels
Indirect — news coverage
Tracked positions — 4 day window
Ticker Keyword At detection Now Change Days tracked

What this is: a personal research feed built on top of a script that watches a defined watchlist for mentions, then reports the price before and after. What this isn't: a trading signal. A confirmed multi-day trend has usually already been priced in by the time it shows up here — this exists to save you from checking the news manually, not to predict anything. Data updates daily via an automated GitHub Actions pipeline.
